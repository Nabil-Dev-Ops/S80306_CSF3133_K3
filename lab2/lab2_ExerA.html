About the Event

Join us for an intensive one-day hands-on workshop focused on HTML5, semantic web markup, and modern web application fundamentals.

Keynote Speaker: Dr. Muhammad Nabil

Dr. Muhammad Nabil is a Senior Lecturer in Software Engineering with over 10 years of experience in full-stack web architectures and open-source systems.

Hands-on Activity: Speed Coding Challenge

Participants will team up in pairs to build responsive, accessible web pages from scratch within 90 minutes. Attractive prizes await the top three teams!
