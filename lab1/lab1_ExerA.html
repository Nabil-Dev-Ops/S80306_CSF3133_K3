Kopi & Roti Café

How to Order

  1. Look at our menu and choose your food and drinks.
  2. Tell our staff your order at the counter.
  3. Pay at the counter (cash or e-wallet).
  4. Wait for your order number to be called.
  5. Enjoy your meal!
