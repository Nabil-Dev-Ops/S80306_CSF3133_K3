About the University

Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT) is a public university located in Kuala Nerus, Terengganu, Malaysia. Previously known as Kolej Universiti Sains dan Teknologi Malaysia, it is associated with the study of marine science and aquatic resources.

The university began as a fisheries and marine science centre of Universiti Pertanian Malaysia. Its location near the South China Sea provided opportunities for education and research related to marine environments.

Over the years, the institution developed into a university college and later became Universiti Malaysia Terengganu. Its work encompasses a range of academic disciplines alongside its established focus on the ocean and sustainability.

Source: Universiti Malaysia Terengganu on Wikipedia
