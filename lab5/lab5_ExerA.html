About the Festival

Taste of Terengganu brings together local food stalls for a celebration of food, music and culture. Visitors can enjoy dishes such as nasi dagang, keropok lekor and laksam, prepared by local cooks and small businesses.

The festival also supports local entrepreneurs and students. Visitors can discover traditional flavours, meet the people behind the stalls and enjoy a lively day with family and friends.

Free entry for all UMT students – show your matric card at the entrance.
